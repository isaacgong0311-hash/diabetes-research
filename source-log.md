## Appraisal note

These sources establish the current clinical and surveillance framework. The next extraction stage should add primary randomized trials and systematic reviews so the final report can quantify absolute benefits, harms, follow-up duration, applicability, and risk of bias rather than relying on recommendations alone.

## Primary-trial extraction added November 10, 2026

The pivotal-evidence modules now add trial-level population, comparator, follow-up, absolute event rates where reported, harms, funding, and applicability limits. The source set includes:

- Prevention and remission: DPP/DPPOS, Finnish DPS, Look AHEAD, DiRECT, and DIADEM-I.
- Weight and glycemia: STEP 2, SURMOUNT-2, SURPASS-2, randomized withdrawal studies, STAMPEDE, and longer-term metabolic-surgery trials and reviews.
- Cardiovascular and kidney outcomes: EMPA-REG OUTCOME, DECLARE–TIMI 58, CREDENCE, DAPA-CKD, EMPA-KIDNEY, LEADER, REWIND, SURPASS-CVOT, FLOW, SELECT, and a large GLP-1 receptor agonist meta-analysis.
- CGM: MOBILE, IMMEDIATE, CONNECT, two meta-analyses, and the FDA Stelo decision summary, with explicit separation of diabetes management, diagnosis, and a behavioral N-of-1 experiment.

See [module 1](pivotal-evidence-prevention-remission.md), [module 2](pivotal-evidence-incretins-surgery.md), [module 3](pivotal-evidence-cardiorenal.md), and [module 4](pivotal-evidence-cgm.md). These are selected evidence extractions, not systematic reviews; database search strategies, duplicate screening, formal risk-of-bias instruments, and certainty grading have not yet been completed.
